Pilot Schedule Bid

Template spreadsheet

Choose the bid packet workbook and the sheet with this year's lines. The finished schedule is written back into this sheet.

Staffing limits

Minimum pilots available (same rule as the sheet's "Pilots Required" rows). A day off can't be picked if it would drop availability below this.

Pilots

Seniority 1 picks first in every round. Type a seniority number to move a pilot, or use the ↑ ↓ buttons. Initials link pilots to their training blocks. Leave an allowance blank to use the default.

Rounds

Round 1 is the line pick. Each round after it gives every pilot a number of V, HAC and holiday days. "Min in a row" is the shortest group of days off a pick can make: a lone single day isn't allowed, but a single day added next to days off the pilot already has is. "Min working days between groups" stops a pick from leaving just one working day between two groups of days off: a pick must either touch other days off or leave at least that many working days. Set either to 1 or 0 to switch it off. The yearly V and HAC allowances under Pilots cap the total across all rounds.

Training

Read from the yellow TRAINING row. Dates are the whole shaded window, from the position day out to the position day back. When a pilot picks a line, their sessions are put on it: P the day before, T on training days, P the day after. If training lands on a day off, it's written as "OFF T" / "OFF P", the same as in 2025.

Holidays, events and safety/training days

Dates as YYYY-MM-DD, separated by spaces or new lines. Events (Board / MT dates) use the event staffing limit. Safety and company training days (marked "Safety Day" or "training" under the dates in the sheet) use the safety & training limit, which is checked first.
